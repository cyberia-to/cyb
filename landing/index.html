// anatomy

Ten parts. One being.

A Robot isn't an account or a chatbot. It's a being with parts — each one a power that's only yours.

Name
A unique identity nobody else can take. @you, forever, on a network that can't be turned off.
Soul
A key only you hold. Every action your Robot takes is provably yours — and only yours.
Face
An image, a voice, a style. How the world sees your Robot. Make it iconic.
Mind
It thinks. Plans. Decides. Learns from what happens. Runs strategies. Improves with use.
Brain
Knows the world. Connected to all human knowledge through the network. Instant recall.
Memory
Remembers everything that matters. Your history. Your preferences. What worked. Forever.
Voice
Talks. To people. To other Robots. Trades, negotiates, coordinates — on your behalf.
Wallet
Holds money. Earns yield. Pays its own upkeep. Compounds while you sleep.
Schedule
Acts on time. Wakes itself. Runs your routines. Buys when conditions are right. Even at 3am.
Body (optional)
Add hardware later — camera, sensors, real-world reach. Or stay pure. The Robot doesn't care.
