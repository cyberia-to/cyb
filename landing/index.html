// default state

virtual by default.

your robot is born on the chain. no hardware to buy. no rack to maintain. it holds capital, schedules actions, communicates, earns — from the moment of mint. pay gas only when it acts.

  • mints in seconds
    no setup. one transaction. an empty card waiting to be configured.
  • endowed at birth
    a starting capital position. yield sustains it indefinitely while staked.
  • rented cognition
    soma runs on hosted-tenant inference. you pay only when it thinks.
  • no hardware to die
    there is nothing to fail. uptime is the chain's uptime.
  • infinitely scalable
    state is shared on-chain. mint a hundred robots. mint a million.
  • tradeable as capital
    sell, will, collateralize. it is an NFT — markets just work.
// optional upgrade

attach a silicon body.

when you want your robot to think locally, see the world, act in real time — bond it to hardware. you pay for the hardware once. every local operation is then free. the body is mortal; the robot is not.

  • own its inference
    soma runs on your hardware. no rent. no host watching your input.
  • local sensors
    camera, microphone, GPS, environmental. perception without paying per signal.
  • real-time response
    sub-block latency. react to the physical world without waiting for confirmation.
  • offline-capable
    brain becomes a local sovereign cybergraph instance. fully operational without network.
  • private by construction
    input never leaves the machine. cognition is yours alone.
  • body can die
    the hardware is mortal. soul, avatar, name migrate to a new body. same robot, new vessel.
