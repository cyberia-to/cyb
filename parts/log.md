# log

the history of every interaction — and it already exists in substance: the durable tade `~/cyb/graph.log` *is* the log. every cast, every answer, every act lands on the tade; nothing is forgotten. [[cyb/parts/time|time]] reads the log backward as the past half of its screen.

the log world is authored in rune (`cells/log.rune`): census (signals, axons, stake) and a table of every cyberlink — step, from, to, token, stake, valence. world-to-world attention hops seed the graph and stay off this tape. a [[cyber/block|block]] is the network's tick; an [[cyber/epoch|epoch]] is a pack of blocks. the commander still runs underneath.
